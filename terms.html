2. Non-Custodial Architecture & 12-Word Recovery Seed Phrase

BOOBA operates under strict cryptographic and non-custodial principles:

  • Master Recovery Phrase: When minting your Booba Passport, you are issued a unique 12-word cryptographic seed phrase (BIP-39 standard). This recovery phrase is your master key to restore access if you forget your credentials.
  • User Sole Responsibility: You are solely responsible for saving, backing up, and safeguarding your 12-word recovery phrase. The BOOBA team does not store, possess, or have the technical ability to recover lost seed phrases.
  • Zero-Knowledge Security: Passwords and seed phrases are hashed cryptographically using SHA-256 before verification. The team will never ask for your 12-word recovery phrase or private keys.
